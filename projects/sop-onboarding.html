Business Process Improvement

Reducing Employee Onboarding Time Through Process Standardisation

Restaurant Kitchen Operations – SOP Development

A real-world initiative to solve high employee turnover challenges and build a consistent, scalable training system.

👔 Role: Assistant Manager 👥 Team Size: 9 members 📅 Period: Oct 2022 – Present 📍 Location: Finland

Project Overview

1

The Opportunity

High employee turnover in the kitchen team led to long onboarding time, inconsistent training and operational inefficiencies.

  • Heavy dependency on experienced staff
  • Knowledge existed only in people's heads
  • Inconsistent training methods
  • New staff took ~3 months to work independently
2

My Role

I identified the root causes and designed a structured SOP and onboarding framework to standardise work processes and accelerate training.

  • Observed daily operations & pain points
  • Interviewed experienced staff
  • Documented workflows & best practices
  • Created SOP, checklists & training plan
3

The Impact

  • 66% Reduced onboarding time (3 months → 1 month)
  • Consistent training quality and food standard
  • Less Dependency on individual experienced staff
  • Higher Retention and better experience for new employees
