COKE COFFEE CAMPAIGN

Social Listening & Campaign Insight Analysis

📋 Social Listening 📅 Feb 21 - Mar 25, 2018 👤 Research & Analysis Team 📍 HCMC, Vietnam

Analytical Framework & Processes

Three structured processes were applied to transform raw social data into actionable business recommendations for Coca-Cola Vietnam.

1

Social Listening & Campaign Insight Process

Purpose: Transform social data into actionable insights to support marketing and business decisions.

🎯

1. Define Objectives

  • Understand campaign objectives & KPIs
  • Identify key research questions
📥

2. Data Collection

  • Collect social data from multiple sources
  • Filter data by keywords, brand, time period
⚙️

3. Data Processing

  • Clean and categorize data
  • Remove noise & irrelevant mentions
  • Tag data by theme, channel, sentiment
📊

4. Analysis

  • Analyze buzz volume & trends
  • Sentiment analysis
  • Channel effectiveness analysis
  • Identify key topics & drivers
💡

5. Insights & Findings

  • Synthesize key insights
  • Identify opportunities and risks
  • Provide strategic recommendations
📝

6. Reporting & Communication

  • Build structured report & visualizations
  • Deliver executive summary
  • Present to stakeholders
  • Support decision-making
Key Outputs: Insight report · Executive summary · Dashboard · Recommendation
2

Product Launch Evaluation Process

Purpose: Evaluate product launch performance and customer perception to optimize strategy and execution.

📋

1. Launch Plan Alignment

  • Align on launch objectives, target audience & KPIs
  • Define evaluation framework
📡

2. Campaign Monitoring

  • Monitor campaign activities in real-time
  • Track buzz, reach, engagement by channel
📈

3. Performance Analysis

  • Analyze performance by phase, channel, and content
  • Compare vs. benchmarks or norms
👥

4. Customer Perception Analysis

  • Analyze sentiment & emotions
  • Identify key positive & negative topics
  • Voice of Customer analysis
💡

5. Insights & Recommendations

  • Identify what works & what doesn't
  • Provide actionable recommendations
🤝

6. Stakeholder Decision Support

  • Present findings to stakeholders
  • Support decisions on content, channel, and investment allocation
Key Outputs: Performance dashboard · Sentiment score · Top themes · Channel effectiveness report · Strategic recommendations
3

Insight Reporting & Stakeholder Communication Process

Purpose: Deliver clear, actionable insights to stakeholders for effective decision-making and campaign optimization.

✅

1. Data & Insight Validation

  • Validate data accuracy and reliability
  • Ensure insights are consistent and credible
📐

2. Report Structure Development

  • Define report structure and key messages
  • Select relevant charts and visualizations
📊

3. Report Creation

  • Build dashboards & visuals
  • Write insight narratives and recommendations
  • Highlight key takeaways
🔍

4. Quality Review

  • Review insights and data
  • Ensure clarity, accuracy, and actionability
🎤

5. Presentation & Delivery

  • Present to internal team and client
  • Explain insights and recommendations
🔄

6. Feedback & Follow-Up

  • Gather feedback from stakeholders
  • Refine recommendations
  • Monitor implementation and impact
Key Outputs: Executive report · Dashboard · Presentation · Action plan · Follow-up insights

Campaign Overview

MARCH - APRIL 2018 & AUG - SEP 2018

🔴 TEASING (TRIGGER) — 1 Week (21 Feb - 27 Feb)

Objective: Create curiosity, create trend for Coke Coffee

Angles:

  • A new taste of Coke Coffee from Australia
  • A new trend has stormed Vietnam: Coke Coffee

Total: 29 Posts

🟠 LAUNCHING — 3 Weeks (28 Feb - 21 Mar)

Objective: Create Mass Awareness & Deliver Product Intrinsic Benefits

  • Coke Coffee is launched here in Vietnam
  • Build Product Credibility with Taste and Uplift benefits
  • Highlight the whole afternoon with 30% more caffeine from Coke

Total: 22 Posts

🟢 REINFORCING CAMPAIGN — 4 Weeks (The National Taste)

Objective: Reinforce Uplifting message together with National Pride

  • Coke Coffee with the real coffee been from Vietnam — A National Pride
  • "Tự hào hương vị cà phê Việt" to celebrate Coke Coffee — the national taste of Vietnam

Business Impact

📢

Identified that KOL channels generated approximately 41% of campaign buzz, helping prioritise influencer-driven communication strategies.

📊

Revealed significant differences in sentiment across communication channels, enabling optimisation of media investments and campaign messaging.

👥

Identified customer concerns regarding taste profile and pricing perception, providing actionable input for product positioning and future campaign iterations.

🎯

Supported enterprise decision-making for a national product launch through structured consumer intelligence and social listening analysis.

The insights from this campaign analysis helped Coca-Cola Vietnam understand consumer perception, optimise communication strategy and improve future campaign performance.
