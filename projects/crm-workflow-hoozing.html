Business Process Project

CRM Workflow Coordination at Hoozing

Managing end-to-end customer lifecycle through an internal CRM system

At Hoozing, I managed and optimized the entire customer lifecycle workflow in our internally developed CRM system, ensuring smooth collaboration across multiple departments and external partners.

🏢 Company: Hoozing 👤 Role: Sales Account Manager 📅 Jun 2019 - Jun 2022 📍 Vietnam

Project Overview

🔴

Problem

The customer journey involved multiple departments and third-party providers. Information was scattered, causing delays, miscommunication and lack of visibility for management.

💡

My Solution

I managed the end-to-end workflow in the CRM system, standardized processes, ensured data accuracy and improved collaboration across all stakeholders.

✅

Result

Improved workflow transparency, faster response time, more accurate data and higher customer satisfaction.

Business Process Diagram

Customer Lifecycle Workflow

Workflow Detail

Stage Key Activities Stakeholders Involved System Actions
Marketing Lead generation, campaigns Marketing Team Create lead in CRM
CRM Ticket Capture inquiries, qualify leads Marketing, Sales Create ticket, add info
Accept Review and accept ticket Sales Agent Update status to "Accepted"
Assign Sales Assign to best-fit agent Sales Manager Assign owner in CRM
Property Viewing Contact customer, arrange viewing Sales Agent, Customer Log activities, schedule
Legal & Contract Prepare contract, send to customer Legal, Sales, Customer Upload documents, track status
Third-party Services Coordinate utilities, internet, cleaning... Sales, Third-party Partners Create tasks, follow up
After Sales Support Support move-in and after-sales requests Customer Care, Partners Log requests, update status
Completed Close ticket, collect feedback Sales, Customer Close ticket, feedback form
Reporting Generate reports, analyze KPIs Sales Manager, Management Export data, dashboards

My Responsibilities

  • ✔ Managed and coordinated the full customer lifecycle in the CRM system.
  • ✔ Assigned and monitored customer requests from lead to post-sales services.
  • ✔ Collaborated with Marketing, Sales, Legal, Property Owners and third-party providers.
  • ✔ Ensured CRM data accuracy, updated workflow status and maintained communication.
  • ✔ Generated reports and KPIs for management decision-making.
  • ✔ Identified bottlenecks and suggested process improvements.
